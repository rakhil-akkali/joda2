Product Overview

GiftEase is an end-to-end corporate gifting automation platform built for Indian enterprises. From selection to delivery to analytics—everything happens in one place.

How GiftEase Works

  1. Define the OccasionBirthdays, festivals, onboarding, milestones, client campaigns.
  2. Set Rules & BudgetsControl spend, approvals, and recipient logic.
  3. AI Recommends GiftsBased on role, location, preferences, and occasion.
  4. Automate & SendTriggered via HRMS or CRM events.
  5. Track & MeasureDelivery status, spend reports, and campaign impact.
